# VocabEz: 30-Day GregMAT Vocabulary Mastery Blueprint (810 Words)

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Methodology
Mastering all 810 words (27 sets $\times$ 30 words) in 30 days requires an evidence-based cognitive system that combines:
1. **Horizon-Constrained Spaced Repetition (SM-2-30)**: Adapts classical SuperMemo interval curves so reviews occur before Day 30 rather than expanding past the exam deadline.
2. **Leitner 5-Box Spacing**: Words advance through Boxes 1–5 based on active retrieval accuracy.
3. **GregMAT Math Strategy (Polarity Algebra)**: Classifying every word into **Positive (+)**, **Negative (-)**, or **Neutral (0)** charges to crack GRE Text Completion sentences algebraically.
4. **Sentence Equivalence Pairing**: Rapidly linking cross-set synonym twins (e.g., *laconic* [Set 12] $\leftrightarrow$ *taciturn* [Set 1], *specious* [Set 6] $\leftrightarrow$ *spurious* [Set 6], *castigate* [Set 2] $\leftrightarrow$ *lambaste* [Set 27]).
5. **Morphological Root Clumping**: Grouping by Latin/Greek roots (`-loqu-`, `-path-`, `-bell-`, `-plac-`, `-luc-`, `-ver-`).

## 3. The 6 Practice Modes Available Anytime
1. **GregMAT Math Strategy**: Practice classifying words into +, -, or 0.
2. **Sentence Equivalence Pairing**: Find twin synonyms across sets.
3. **GRE Sentence Completion**: Fill in the blank using transition words (`although`, `despite`, `furthermore`).
4. **Speed Sprint Blitz**: 60-second rapid-fire active recall.
5. **Weakest Words Hospital**: Zero hearts deducted! Triage words you've missed until accuracy hits 100%.
6. **SM-2 Spaced Repetition Due**: Review cards exactly when cognitive memory decay begins.
